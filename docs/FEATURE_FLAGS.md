# Feature switches and their defaults

Every switch is per character unless it says account. "Off" switches send and record nothing until the player turns them on.

| Feature | Where | Default | What turning it on does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome recorder (After action, Chronicle, timings) | After action page | **off** | Records what happened in your sessions (money, quests, levels, travel) on this PC |
| Sharing (hello, rares, gathering, kit cards) | Sharing page, `/ride share` | **off** | Sends those to guild or party addon messages |
| Group plan | Sharing, Group plan | **off** | Sends your goals, plan picks and ready to your party |
| World observation export | `ns.Contribute` (account) | **off** | Lets you preview and export your world observations as text |
| Planner (Plan page) | Plan page | off until switched on | Builds a plan from Outrider's sources |
| Lessons | Class, Lessons | on | Offers short tips out of combat; Turn lessons off stops them |
| Experience mode | Settings, Look, `/ride mode` | Beginner for a new character, Experienced otherwise | Changes how much a card says, never what is known |
| Foresight nudges | Plan page | off | Short reminders about things coming up soon |
| Macro approval | Macros page | always | An update shows a preview first; only the previewed text is installed |
