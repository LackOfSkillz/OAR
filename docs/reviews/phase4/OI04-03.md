# OI04-03: Session ending strategy

**Requirement:** reserve time for hand-ins, repairs, a safe rest point and next-session preparation when applicable.

**Acceptance:** a near-deadline re-plan offers a feasible close and never starts an impossible long step; no automatic logout or action.

## What was built

`Intelligence/SessionEnd.lua`, used by Horizon and NexusCore.

**Session clock.** It starts when you set a session length; the same length set again keeps the clock, a new length restarts it. The planner's budget is the time left minus a closing reserve:
- **Repairs:** below 30% durability, the trip to the nearest repair vendor plus a minute.
- **Hand-ins:** 60 s for each complete quest in your log.
- **Rest point:** the trip to the nearest inn, when you chose "End at a rest point".

**Closing time.** This is the last 10 minutes, or the last quarter of a shorter session. The plan then holds only closing steps: "Repair before you stop", "Hand in N finished quests", "Head to an inn to stop". It checks them against the time left with the planner's margin, so a long step is never started. With nothing that fits, it says "not enough time left for anything: a good moment to stop" (`session_over`), and the Plan page shows that the session is nearly over.

**Next-session preparation** is covered by Recovery (OI03-13) and NextSession (OI03-12); nothing new was needed here.

**Nothing is automatic.** Closing steps are waypoints or pages, used by your click. A test checks the file has no logout, quit, cast or use call.

## Evidence

`tests/spec/sessionend_spec.lua`, 6 tests:
- the reserve's parts
- the budget is the time left minus the reserve
- near the end, closing steps only, within the time left
- "good moment to stop" when nothing fits
- a short session isn't closing from the start, and nothing logs out or acts
- the clock restarts only on a new length

Related specs pass: horizon, multiobjective.
