# OI04-10: Foresight triggers

**Requirement:** upcoming trainer, quest, mount, material and dungeon needs, based on supported prerequisites and the player's goals; reuse Radar and Handoffs.

**Acceptance:** trigger fixtures include near-route savings and false positives; unknown facts are not alerts; no automatic quest acceptance.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Foresight.lua`. `Evaluate(facts)` is pure; `Facts()` reads what the addon already knows:

| Trigger | Facts used | Raised when |
|---|---|---|
| trainer | next training level (even levels, as Errands); nearest class trainer (Nearest) | that level is within 60 min at your pace; "on your way" plus the trip it saves only when the trainer's map is on the plan's route |
| quests | the zone's locked quests "level N" (NPCPack) | within 60 min at your pace |
| riding | the cited levelling fact (riding at 40 for 100 g); your gold minus the goal reserve | within 4 levels: "short" or "enough" |
| materials | the craft queue's shopping list (CraftQueue) | anything is missing |
| dungeons | DungeonGuide "soon" | a dungeon opens within its soon window |
| alts | Handoffs (BoE upgrades your alts hold) | up to 2 |

**Withheld, not alerted:** when a key fact is unknown (your pace, the riding cost, your gold), the trigger is withheld with the reason.

**Pace:** comes from complete XP sessions (`GearROI.Pace`); unknown pace means no timed triggers.

**Never acts:** nothing accepts a quest, buys, trains or casts, and a test checks the file for those calls.

**Display:** it appears quietly in OI04-15 (one primary, two secondary, cooldowns, no combat).

`tests/spec/foresight_spec.lua`, 6 tests:

- false positives: too far ahead, and unknown timing withheld
