# OI04-13: Pathfinder measured routes

**Requirement:** feed learned travel times into Travel and Journey with their source, version and route deviation; don't treat an untraveled straight line as a valid road.

**Acceptance:** tests cover disconnected edges, map changes, no history, slow mode, a wrong build and the fallback; the route stays player-directed.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Pathfinder.lua`. At login it wraps `Travel:TimeTo` once and keeps the original as `TimeToRaw`. Every caller then gets the corrected seconds (WhatNow quest trips, Journey, the planner, GearROI, Foresight, SessionEnd), plus a third return value describing the estimate.

**Correction**, the first that applies:
- **This route:** your walking trips on this route (start map to destination map), median of actual ÷ estimate, at least 3 trips.
- **Your walking:** all your walking trips, at least 5.
- **Fallback:** Travel's plain estimate.

Only trips from the current game build count, so a patch or a redrawn map starts fresh. Every estimate carries its source, sample count and build.

**Straight lines:** Travel's last leg is a straight run from the nearest known point. When that leg is over 120 s and the route hasn't been walked, the estimate is marked "straight line: no known road, it may take longer". No known way stays unknown: never a straight line.

**Slow mode:** a per-character setting multiplies by 1.6, for walking without a mount or as a ghost.

**Deviation:** `Deviation()` gives how far your real trips were from the plan (median ratio, count).

**No self-measurement:** TravelTimes records the raw estimate (`TimeToRaw`), so the measurement never measures itself.

**Player-directed:** nothing sets a waypoint or moves you, and a test checks this.
