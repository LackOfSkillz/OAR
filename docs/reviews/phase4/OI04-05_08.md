# OI04-05 to OI04-08: GearROI (baseline, acquisition, replacement, utility)

All four are in `Intelligence/GearROI.lua` (pure functions, no store). It reuses GearScore (role-weighted % against what you wear, usability), Nearby (sources), Travel (trips), SessionXP (pace), Ledger (gold rate) and the goals' reserve.

| Task | What it does | Acceptance and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| OI04-05 baseline | `Baseline`: improvement %, slot, usable now / at level N / not at all with the reason, binding, and source type (guaranteed: quest, vendor, crafted, alt, auction; chance: drop, object). The score is labelled "role score from your stat weights, not a DPS figure". | guaranteed vs chance; class, level, bind and slot eligibility; the no-DPS-claim note (2 tests) |
| OI04-06 time and risk | `Acquisition`: trip plus activity (quest: your time or Journey's 300 s; vendor and alt: 1 min; crafted when known). Drops have no time without a chance. With a chance: expected tries 1/p, `ChanceWithin` = 1-(1-p)^n, "assumes independent rolls", a group share p/g, "drops only while you have its quest", "whether you'll find a group is unknown". Zero chance is impossible; an expected time is flagged as expected, never guaranteed. | zero and unknown, conditional, independence, group share and availability, guaranteed sources (3 tests) |
| OI04-07 replacement horizon | `Replacement`: the next upgrade for the same slot at a higher level from known sources, and the hours until you reach that level at your pace (`Pace`, from complete verified sessions, at least 30 min of data). No known upgrade: a typical 5-level span as a range. Unknown pace: no hours, with the reason. The item itself and other slots never count, so no circular use. | next upgrade and hours, unknown pace, range, pace from valid sessions (2 tests) |
| OI04-08 utility | `Utility`: (improvement × useful hours) per hour spent getting it. Gold counts as hours at your gold rate. Anything costing more than you can spend above the goal reserve is out. Unknown time, life or gold rate gives the reason instead of a number, and `Rank` puts unknowns after known. Units: "upgrade-hours per hour spent". | an expensive short-lived item ranks below a free one; reserve; unknown reasons; units; live `Mine` runs cleanly (3 tests) |

Evidence: `tests/spec/gearroi_spec.lua`, 10 tests.
