# OI04-15: Foresight quiet UX

**Requirement:** opt-in categories, a cooldown, duplicate suppression, an importance threshold, quiet mode and a post-combat recheck.

**Acceptance:** one primary plus two secondary notes; no interruptions in combat; dismiss and snooze persist; no irrelevant repeat spam.

## What was built (in `Intelligence/Foresight.lua`)

**Passive display.** The Plan page has a "Coming up" section with one primary card ("coming up") and up to two secondary cards ("also"). Nothing pops up.

**Per character** (`char.foresight`):
- **Categories:** a category you switch off is left out (trainer, quest, mount, material, dungeon, alt).
- **Threshold:** notes below it are left out (default 0.45).
- **Not interested:** the note never comes back.
- **Later:** hides the note for a day.

**Optional nudges in the Feed.** These are off by default (`notify`):
- only important notes (0.7 or more)
- each note at most once per 6 hours
- never in combat or in quiet mode
- re-checked after combat ends, on a level-up and on a new zone

Feed entries for the same note also merge, which is the existing behaviour.

## Evidence

- `tests/spec/foresight_spec.lua`, 4 new tests (10 in total):
  - one primary plus two, with threshold and categories
  - dismiss and snooze persist, and a snooze comes back
  - nudges are off by default, important only, once per cooldown, never in combat or quiet, and re-checked after combat
  - the Plan page shows Coming up with tooltipped buttons
- `tips_spec` and `nexus_page_spec` pass.
