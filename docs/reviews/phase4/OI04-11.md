# OI04-11: Quartermaster early reservations

**Requirement:** explicit material and gold hold indicators for vendor and gear advice and the account-wide inventory; the full allocator comes later.

**Acceptance:** reserved stock can't be counted twice; no blocking of item transfer or sale without clear UI and an override; stale alt inventory is flagged.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Quartermaster.lua`.

**Holds**
- **Material holds:** your explicit item holds (`char.quartermaster.items`, with a purpose), then your craft queue's needs (`CraftQueue:Needed`).
- **Gold held:** the goals' reserve plus an explicit gold hold.

**`Allocate(holds, stock)`** (pure): each unit is counted once across all holds.
- Order: your own bags and bank first, then your alts' saved inventories.
- An alt's count older than 7 days is flagged stale.
- What a hold can't cover is "short".

**Indicators**
- Item tooltips (Inventory) gain "Held: N for <purpose>", with "(N short)" and "(an alt's count is over a week old)" when they apply.
- GearROI's spendable gold now excludes all held gold, not only the goal reserve.

**Nothing is blocked or moved.** Selling or mailing a held item stays your choice; the tooltip is the clear UI and the override is simply going ahead. A test checks the file makes no item or mail calls. The full allocator is Phase 5 (Quartermaster, OI05).

## Evidence

- `tests/spec/quartermaster_spec.lua`, 5 tests:

  - explicit and craft-queue holds, in order
  - the tooltip line, with no move or sell calls
  - held gold
- `inventory_spec` and `gearroi_spec` pass.
