# OI04-04: Horizon live adaptation

**Requirement:** re-plan on meaningful progress or detours, keep a pinned plan unless it becomes infeasible, and offer a manual alternate.

**Acceptance:** late events, pause and resume, a new goal, budget exhaustion and combat suppression are tested; no thrashing.

## What already held

- **Progress and detours:** finished quests, zone changes, gear, gold and so on move the state revision (State.lua events, OI02-R17), so the planner re-plans after a debounce.
- **Combat:** a request in combat waits for combat to end.
- **Pause:** while paused there is no re-plan; the plan is marked stale.
- **Pin:** the pin is a planner input, and an infeasible pin is reported (`pin_gone` or `pin_infeasible`).
- **Budget exhaustion:** handled by the session ending (OI04-03, `session_over`).

## What changed

**No thrashing (NexusCore).**
- **Spacing:** automatic re-plans (from game events) are at least 20 s (`MIN_GAP`) after the last one.
- **Stability:** an automatic re-plan keeps your current first step unless the new best first step scores clearly higher: more than `HYSTERESIS`, which is 10%. The two first steps are compared, not plan totals.
- **Your presses are free:** Re-plan, pin, skip, goals, session settings and resume always re-plan at once (`MANUAL`).

**Manual alternate.** A "Try another start" button pins the best alternative first step and re-plans. It has a tooltip, and says so when there is no other start.

## Evidence

- `tests/spec/liveadapt_spec.lua`, 6 tests:
  - an event burst can't re-plan within the gap
  - your presses re-plan at once
  - a small change keeps the first step, a clear improvement switches it, and a manual Re-plan is free
  - a pinned plan stays, and an impossible pin is reported
  - Try another start
  - combat, pause and late events: nothing during, one re-plan after
- Updated: two `nexus_core_spec` tests now wait the minimum gap; this is the intended behaviour.
- Related: `sessionend_spec` covers budget exhaustion; the Phase 2 review specs pass.
