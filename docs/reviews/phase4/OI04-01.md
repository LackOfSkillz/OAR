# OI04-01: Horizon session inputs

**Requirement:** a bounded session budget (15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes, or custom), solo, group or either, goals, reserve and stop preferences.

**Acceptance:** inputs validate; "no group" excludes group prerequisites; existing saved preferences migrate; no LLM required.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Horizon.lua` saves its settings with the planner (`horizon`):

| Setting | Values | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| minutes | 15, 30, 60, 120, a custom whole number from 5 to 480, or none | the planner's time budget (`ctx.budgetSeconds`); unknown step times count as 900 s against it (Phase 2) |
| group | solo, group, either | solo: steps that need a group (dungeons, dungeon quests) are invalid with "needs a group (you chose solo)"; through the graph's prerequisite spread, so is anything that needs them first |
| stopRest | on or off | end the session at a rest point; used by the session ending (OI04-03) |

**Goals and reserve:** these are unchanged; they live in the existing goal editor (OI02-R07).

**Validation:** bad values are refused with the reason. A damaged saved horizon is kept aside (`ns.KeepBad`) and the default is used.

**Migration:** when no horizon is saved yet, the minutes you set elsewhere are copied in: the Ask snapshot's `askMinutes`, or a Radar budget you changed from its default of 45. The old settings stay where they are.

**Stale plan:** the horizon is part of the planning context key, so changing it makes the current plan stale at once.

**Plan page:** a new "This session" section shows the current horizon in words, for example "30 min, solo". It has buttons for 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours and No limit; for Solo, With a group and Either; and an "End at a rest point" switch. Every button has a tooltip.

**No language model, no provider:** it is all local logic.
