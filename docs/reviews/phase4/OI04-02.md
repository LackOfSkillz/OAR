# OI04-02: Multi-objective planning

**Requirement:** use Nexus to combine quests, gear, gathering, errands, travel and turn-ins; merge overlapping objectives.

**Acceptance:** a fixture proves shared travel and rewards are counted once and prerequisites are respected; time bounds are shown as estimates with a buffer.

## What already held (Phase 2)

- Every producer feeds one plan: quests, gear, gathering, errands, dungeons, gold, rares, the route, deals and handoffs.
- The same quest from two producers is one reward (the overlap group: counted benefit once, quest-key dedup).
- Prerequisites, including any-of, come first.

## What changed

- **Activity time counts:** a step's time is now its trip plus its own activity estimate when the producer gives one (`estimatedActivitySeconds`, such as Journey's 300 s per quest). Before, only the trip counted.
- **Shared travel counted once:** `Planner.StepSeconds` handles a step on the same map as the step before. Its trip from your starting point is replaced by a short local move (`LOCAL_MOVE`, 120 s, capped at the trip itself).
- **Margin:** plans report `estimateSeconds` and `bufferedSeconds` (estimate × 1.2). A time budget is checked against the margined figure.
- **Plan page:** it says "About N min for these steps (an estimate; allow up to M min). Travel shared between steps on one map is counted once."

## Evidence

- `tests/spec/multiobjective_spec.lua`, 5 tests:
  - several kinds in one plan
  - activity time counted
  - shared travel counted once, with the exact figure and margin checked
  - one reward offered twice counts once, with the prerequisite first
  - the budget is checked with the margin, and the page states the estimate
- Updated: `horizon_spec` now checks the plan's own margined estimate.
- Related specs pass: `nexus_planner_spec`, `nexus_bench_spec`.
