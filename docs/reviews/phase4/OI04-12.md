# OI04-12: Guardian travel advice

**Requirement:** use verified level and hazard facts, and historical deaths where available, to label risk and offer safer alternatives.

**Acceptance:** no hidden enemy detection or secret-derived calculations; missing risk data is labelled; the player controls the route choice.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Guardian.lua`.

**Facts used:**
- **Zone level range:** from ZoneLevels, which states its source: creatures you saw, a guide, or the Classic pack.
- **Your deaths:** recorded per map (Nearest's death history).

**Labels:**
- **safe:** your level is at or above the range's top.
- **fair:** inside the range.
- **risky:** 1 to 2 levels under the bottom.
- **dangerous:** 3 or more under.
- **Deaths:** two or more recorded deaths there raise the label one step.
- **No data:** "unknown: no level data for this zone".

**Plan cards:** a risky or dangerous step gets a warning tag and "Safer: <title>" from the plan's own alternatives. "Why" shows the reason: the range, its source, your level and your deaths there. The plan itself is never changed; which way you go is your choice.

**Not used:** no nameplates, unit reactions or combat log, and nothing computed from secret values. A test checks the file for these.
