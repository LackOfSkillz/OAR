# OI04-09: Gear comparison UX

**Requirement:** show current vs candidate, cost, time, risk, lifespan, alternatives and evidence detail on the existing Gear page and tooltips.

**Acceptance:** no purchase action except the existing user-confirmed flow; the player can dismiss and pin; the display works with a missing price and no provider.

## What was built

The Gear page has a new "Worth getting?" section (`GearROI.Cards` over `GearROI:Mine()`). Each card shows:

**Tags**
- +N% versus what you wear
- guaranteed, or a chance (a drop)
- the price, "price unknown" for a vendor or auction item with none, or "no gold"
- about N min, or time unknown
- useful for about N h, or useful life unknown
- kept on top, when you pinned it

**The card's text**
- the worth in "upgrade-hours per hour spent", or "Not ranked:" with the reason
- the chance notes (independence, group share, quest-only)
- the next upgrade for the slot, or why the useful life is unknown
- other options for the same slot
- the role-score note

**Buttons:** Set waypoint (the existing arrow), Keep on top / Unpin, and Hide this one, saved per character in `char.gearROI`. Every button has a tooltip.

**No buy button.** Buying stays in the auction house's own user-confirmed flow (GearShop / AHBuy), and the tests check that no card offers a purchase. Everything works with no price and with no companion or advice file.
