# OI05-03 to OI05-09: the Forge (`Intelligence/Forge.lua`, version `forge-1`)

The Forge sits on top of TalentSim, which already reads each spell from its tooltip, removes current talents before applying a build's, adds auto attack and keeps dummy results. The Forge adds the pieces below without inventing coefficients (rule 7). What it can't model is listed in `F.NOT_MODELLED` and shown with every result.

| Task | What it does | Acceptance covered by `forge_spec` |
|---|---|---|
| OI05-03 model | `Ability(spell)`: cast, cooldown, average, damage over time, heal, cost and resource (C_Spell.GetSpellPowerCost); source "tooltip" | source kept; not-modelled list present; nil-safe |
| OI05-04 timing | `Timeline`: global cooldown, cast times, cooldowns, damage over time not recast while ticking and clipped to the fight; mana only when pool and regen are known | cooldown counts; DoT share; out of mana; "not modelled" note otherwise |
| OI05-05 damage | crit from the client; `Range` widens the low end when hit (9%) or armour (35%, physical) is unknown; resistance named, never applied | range and reasons |
| OI05-06 roles | separate damage, healing with how long mana lasts, survival as time to die at assumed incoming rates (Classic armour formula, marked unverified) | healing has no damage figure; assumption text |
| OI05-07 runner | `Run`: deterministic, at most 1000 steps, refused in combat, partial report | bounded, repeatable, combat refusal |
| OI05-08 compare | `Compare(a, b)`: same model, eligibility and ownership separate, respec cost unknown unless given; nothing spent or equipped | flags and limits |
| OI05-09 dummy | `Calibrate`: same build, fight length within 30%, same buff count; fewer than 3 fights is "not enough to judge"; median against the range | like-for-like filter; verdicts |

`Live(kind)` builds the runner input from the client and fails soft when nothing is readable (tested).

**Known limits:** procs, talents that change spells, rage, energy and combo-point rates, and haste changes aren't modelled. The 9% and 35% widths are conservative bounds, not measured values. Status: MOCK_GREEN, no live evidence.
