# OI05-12 to OI05-16: supply, reservations, budget, realized economics, profession costs

| Task | File | What it does | Tests (`supply_spec`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| OI05-12 supply graph | `Intelligence/Supply.lua` `Expand` | Recipes you know that name their product form a dependency graph. One shared pool means each unit is allocated once. Crafts round up and the extra goes back into the pool. A recipe loop is reported as a cycle. Other-realm and other-faction alts are excluded with the reason, old counts are flagged stale, and every transfer says to check the item isn't soulbound (bind state isn't recorded). | expansion, rounding, cycle, double allocation, realm, faction, stale |
| OI05-13 reservations and transfers | `Supply.Reserve/Release/Active/Plan` | Account-wide reservations (`global.supply.reservations`) are ordered by override, then priority, then age. `Plan` adds the craft queue (priority 50) and returns transfers, a priced shopping list and the craft sequence. Releasing marks a reservation released and keeps it. | priority and override, release kept, stock change re-plans, no mail, craft or buy calls (static) |
| OI05-14 Treasurer | `Intelligence/Treasurer.lua` | Whole-copper reserves: training (trainer costs seen), riding (guide fact, cleared once well past its level), gear (goal reserve), profession (priced shopping list; unpriced counted), repair (unknown). Your own amount wins. Wallets per character, yours first. `Fits(spend)` answers only whether a spend fits in what isn't kept. | integer copper, unknowns, override, short, wallets |
| OI05-15 realized economics | `Intelligence/Market.lua` `Realized/Compare/SaleChance` | Only confirmed sale invoices count; one with an unknown cut has no net. Unverified (disappeared) auctions are counted apart. Vendor is exact; the auction house shows median, samples and days ("thin" under 3), a cut measured from your sales or unknown, and an unknown deposit. Market depth is always "not known". Sale chance needs at least 5 posts. | all of these |
| OI05-16 profession costs | `Market.Paths` | Recipe colours from the client. Orange gives a sure point with a cost per point; yellow and green are "chance unknown"; grey comes last. Owned reagents count at their price (not free) and unpriced reagents are listed. Observed skill-ups from Outcomes are reported apart. | ordering, owned units, unpriced, observed |

New saved datasets have rows in `docs/quality/data_preservation_audit.json`: `supplyReservations` and `treasurerReserves`.

**Limits:**
- Posts per item aren't recorded yet, so sale chance is usually unknown.
- Yellow and green skill-up rates aren't readable.
- Recipe yield comes from the client; it is marked unknown when the client doesn't give it.
