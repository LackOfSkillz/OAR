# OI02-10 Bounded sequence planner

Date 2026-11-24. New `Intelligence/Planner.lua` (`ns.NexusPlanner.Plan(graph, ranked, ctx, opts)`). It runs a beam search over positively scored nodes:

- at most 60 candidates; a chosen step's prerequisites are pulled in with it;
- horizon 8 and beam width 16;
- a work cap on state expansions (20000).

A plan respects prerequisites (earlier in the plan or already done), exclusions, the spendable gold and an optional time budget. Unknown step time counts as 900 s, not zero.

When the cap is hit, the best plan found so far is returned with `truncated = true` and the reason code `search_capped`. With nothing feasible there is no plan and a plain reason: no worthwhile step, nothing fits the gold, or nothing fits the time and gold. A sequence is never made up.

The result is a validated v4 Plan (steps, reason codes, revision), with its value, up to three alternative first steps, and the work count. Same input, same plan.

Tests: `nexus_planner_spec` (4):
- two small graphs match an exhaustive subset oracle, with prerequisites ordered first;
- a 300-node prerequisite chain terminates under a 50-expansion cap and keeps the horizon of 8;
- infeasible gold and time budgets give no plan with a reason;
- alternatives and determinism.

Full suite green.
