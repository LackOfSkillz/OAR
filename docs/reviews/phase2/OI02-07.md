# OI02-07 Candidate adapters: resources

Date 2026-11-24. Extends `Intelligence/Adapters.lua`:

- **WhatNow's resource producers:** gear (Nearby, GearScore, DungeonGuide), training (ProfTrainer, MoneyGuide), rares (Sightings), gold (MoneyGuide, LootLog) and gather (LootLog, Travel).
- **DealDesk:** live vendor-flip lots from the player's own auction searches. The profit is a measured copper amount.
- **Handoffs:** BoE upgrades held by another character, from saved inventories.
- `A:All()` merges progression and resources into one de-duplicated set.

Every cost or gain is in copper (`costCopper`) or seconds (`costSeconds`), each record carries an evidence id, and the producer's confidence becomes `risk`. No adapter starts a scan or an auction search; the test counts SendBrowseQuery calls and expects 0. Producers are independent: a broken or disabled one is reported unavailable and the others still arrive.

Travel providers appear through the gold, gather and dungeon producers, which already use Travel. A separate travel opportunity is not invented.

Tests: `nexus_resources_spec` (4): five resource kinds with units and evidence, a broken or disabled producer, deal lots with no searches started, All() with unique ids. Full suite green.
