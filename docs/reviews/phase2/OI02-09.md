# OI02-09 Scoring and trade-offs

Date 2026-11-24. New `Intelligence/Score.lua` (`ns.NexusScore`, version `nexus-score-1`):

utility = goalFit x countedBenefit - 0.3 timeCost - 0.3 goldCost - 0.2 risk - 0.2 uncertainty

- **goalFit:** WhatNow's kind weights, averaged over the active goals by priority (1 = 1.0 down to 5 = 0.2).
- **timeCost:** s / (s + 900).
- **goldCost:** copper / spendable gold.
- **uncertainty:** set by the confidence label, plus 0.1 each for unknown time or unknown travel. Unknown is never counted as zero.
- **Hard constraints win:** invalid graph nodes, anything over the spendable gold, and anything with a gold cost but no gold reading are rejected with a reason.
- Ties break by id. Each result carries a trace whose terms add up to the score (checked to 1e-9).

Tests: `nexus_score_spec` (5): determinism, tie order and trace; monotonicity in benefit, time, gold and confidence; bounds in [-1, 1]; reserve, missing gold reading and invalid nodes; cross-unit normalisation, unknown time and blended goals. Full suite green.
