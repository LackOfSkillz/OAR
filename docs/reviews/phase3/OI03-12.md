# OI03-12: Next-session planner reuse

**Requirement:** connect the existing XP60 offline planner and NextSession to Nexus and the calibrated estimates, keeping the safe fallback.

**Acceptance:** fixtures enforce the time budget, quest dedup, and binding to the right character, session and build, and reject stale plans; offline delivery stays policy-gated.

## What already held (reused unchanged)

`Advice:CheckNextSession` (Advisor/AdviceLoader.lua) already refused:
- a plan for another character
- an expired plan, or one over 24 h
- another game build or level
- steps past 60 minutes, or out of order
- a repeated step id
- untrusted candidates (not in the snapshot)
- any model actions

The delivery policy stays exactly as it was: the plan arrives only as the data-only `Outrider_Advice`.

## What changed

**Session binding (with OI03-01).**
- **The problem:** a `/reload` now leaves the last history record as an unfinished segment, so a valid plan would be refused.
- **The fix:** the source is the last merged play session (`SessionXP:Merged()`), the same as the companion's `merge_segments`. A plan naming a segment id rather than the session is still refused.

**Quest dedup across ids.** One quest listed as `quest:<name>` and `dq:<id>` (matched through the snapshot's questID and `NexusAdapters.QuestKey`) is shown once, with a note.

**The planner's view.** `NextSession.Annotate`:
- A step the Nexus graph says can't be done now (wrong faction, missing prerequisite, over budget) is taken out of the timeline, and a note gives the reason.
- Steps that are also in your current plan are marked.

**Calibrated estimates.** With observed quest times for your level band (Estimator, OI03-09), a note says "Your quests take about N min (k done)". If the plan's steps are much shorter than that, it adds "This plan may be optimistic for you".

**Safe fallback.** With no offline plan the window still says "Keep your current route". It now adds a few ideas from the planner or What now?, labelled "not a plan".

**Data preservation.** NextSession replaced the per-character suppression list with an empty one after 80 entries. It now archives the old list first (`xp60Suppressed`, in the audit) and keeps it if archiving fails.
