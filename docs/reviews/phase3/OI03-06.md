# OI03-06: Economy outcome capture

**Requirement:** separate actual gold flow from estimated loot value; track confirmed transactions, and fees, deposits and costs when observable.

**Acceptance:** auctions that disappear are not sales; transfers are not double counted, and estimates are never counted as realized profit; unknown fees are explicit.

## What was built

`Intelligence/EconomyOutcomes.lua` is driven by the outcome recorder (same opt-in switch, same store).

**Realized flow.** Each money_in or money_out outcome is the real change in your gold (PLAYER_MONEY). It now carries a category instead of only the open window. The category comes from the Ledger's window context plus what your own click just did, noted by after-the-fact hooks that only observe:

| Category | When |
|---|---|
| auction sale (confirmed) | mail money taken from a sale invoice (`GetInboxInvoiceInfo` "seller") |
| transfer in / transfer out | mail money from, or gold sent to, one of your own characters. Not profit; sending includes the postage. |
| auction deposit | auction-house money out within 3 s of posting (`C_AuctionHouse.PostItem/PostCommodity`) |
| auction purchase | other auction-house money out |
| vendor sale / vendor spend, training, flight, bank, loot and quests, mail (other), other | from the open window |

**Confirmed sales.** Each sale invoice seen in the mailbox is kept: price, deposit returned and the auction house's cut. They are deduplicated across mailbox refreshes, kept up to 200, and archived as `confirmedSales` (in the preservation audit).
- A cut the client doesn't give is `feeUnknown`, never assumed to be zero.
- Two identical sales read in the same hour count once: this can undercount, never overcount.

**Not sales.** An auction that only disappeared from your list stays "unverified" in AuctionHelper's ledger: it may have expired or been cancelled. The summary counts unverified auctions separately.

**Estimates apart.** `Summary()` keeps three things separate:
- realized flow by category, and realized net excluding transfers
- transfers
- confirmed sales, with known fees and the number of sales with an unknown fee

LootLog's estimated loot value appears only as `estimatedLoot`, flagged as an estimate.

## Found on the way (fixed in its own commit)

`Compat.Call` returns at most five values. The new invoice reader needed the sixth and seventh, and four existing modules had been silently reading nil past the fifth:
- Census: names from a player's GUID
- Favor: reputation value
- LootRolls: whether you can Need or Greed
- PetStatus: autocast on

`Compat.CallAll` keeps every value; those sites now use it. `tests/spec/compat_callall_spec.lua` checks the behaviour and statically rejects any `Compat.Call` that reads more than five values. It was confirmed to catch the old Census line.
