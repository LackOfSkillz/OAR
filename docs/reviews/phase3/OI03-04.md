# OI03-04: Quest time observations

**Requirement:** capture a quest's start and finish (and its prerequisites, when observable); keep multi-session, abandoned, grouped and censored quests separate.

**Acceptance:** no invented durations; samples split by context and build; compared against Journey's 300-second baseline.

## Reuse, not a new store

QuestInfo already keeps a quest diary per character (`global.questHistory[character]`, archived past 600 rows as `questDiary`), with took, handed-in and abandoned rows and their times.
- New rows also carry the play session and segment (`ps`, `seg`), whether you were in a group (`grp`) and the game build (`gb`).
- These are added fields; older rows lack them and are read as "context unknown".
- `Intelligence/QuestTimes.lua` derives everything from the diary on demand and never changes it.

## Sample kinds

| Kind | Meaning | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| complete | took and handed in within one play session | wall time between the two; the trip there and back is included, and inactive time may be (flagged) |
| multi | took and handed in in different play sessions (or, for old rows, more than 4 h apart) | none: logged-out time can't be told from play |
| abandoned | took, then abandoned | none |
| open | not finished yet | none (right-censored) |
| censored | finished with no "took" seen | none (left-censored) |

**Prerequisites:** the quest pack's prerequisites already reach the planner through Nexus metadata (OI02-R02 and R14). The diary doesn't need to copy them.

**Summaries** are split by level band (5 levels), solo or grouped, and game build. Median, p25 and p75 come from complete samples only. A stratum with fewer than 5 samples is marked not enough.

**Comparison** with Journey's `QUEST_TIME` (300 s "once there") happens only for strata with enough samples. It notes that these samples include travel, so a higher figure is expected; nothing is "corrected" from it here. The estimator in OI03-09 decides what to do with it.
