# OI03-11: AfterAction report

**Requirement:** show observed wall-clock XP/hour, realized gold, an activity breakdown, uncertainty, milestones and an alternative focus for the next session.

**Acceptance:** report totals reconcile with their sources; no fake improvement claims; missing or partial sessions are labelled; example screenshots are clearly marked as demo data.

## What was built

`Intelligence/AfterAction.lua` adds a new Home sub-tab, **After action**. `Report(playSessionId)` reads each number from its source:

| Part | Source |
|---|---|
| XP per hour | the XP recorder's merged play session, wall-clock (reload segments joined, OI03-01) |
| Gold | that session's money outcomes through `EconomyOutcomes.Summary`: realized net, transfers between your characters apart, confirmed sales with known cut or unknown cut, loot value shown as an estimate |
| Time | the activity timeline's totals for that session's segments, including the open one |
| Deaths and milestones | that session's outcomes and chronicle entries |
| How long things take you | Estimator over your quest times (current level band, solo; prior Journey 300 s) and walking-trip ratios, with the range, sample count and basis; "not enough yet" when sparse |
| Compared | your own earlier complete sessions (at least 10 min, at least 3 of them), as plain numbers |
| Next time | the planner's picks, or What now?'s, labelled "Ideas for next time (not a prediction)" |

**Partial sessions are labelled:**
- in progress
- cut short (no logout seen)
- XP not fully readable
- time not fully readable
- N parts (reloads)

**No claims:** the text never says "improved", "because" or "thanks to", and a test checks every line for those words.

**Example data:** "Show an example" fills the page with demo values. The page then shows a gold "Example data: not your play." line, and the XP labels say "example data", for screenshots and first looks.

**Controls:**
- "Record outcomes (private, opt-in)" switches the recorder.
- "Export the chronicle" opens the copy dialog. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Every control has a tooltip, and the page has its tip and a Help entry (USER_GUIDE rebuilt).

LootLog gained a public `SessionValue()` so the report doesn't read its internals.
