# OI03-09: Estimator and calibration

**Requirement:** robust median or quantile estimates, or conservative shrinkage, by class, level and activity; minimum sample counts, recency and fallback estimates.

**Acceptance:** a held-out test prevents training leakage; outlier, sparse and patch-change cases widen the interval or fall back; report the actual calibration error.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Estimator.lua` is a set of pure functions over samples and keeps no store. A sample is `{ value, at, build, key }`; the key is the stratum, for example the level band and solo or group for quests. Classes are separate because every store is per character.

**`FitOne(samples, opts)`**
- **Patch change:** only samples from the current game build count. With fewer than 5 of those, older builds are used with the interval widened 1.5× (basis "older build").
- **Recency:** samples from the last 30 days are used when there are at least 5.
- **Outliers:** values more than 5 × MAD from the median are set aside. If more than a tenth were set aside, the interval is widened 1.5×.
- **Sparse:** with fewer than 5 samples it falls back to the prior (for example Journey's 300 s) with a ±50% band, or to basis "none" with no value. Either way it says why.
- **Shrinkage:** with enough samples the estimate is the median shrunk toward the prior by 5 pseudo-samples, so a few odd quests can't move it far.
- **Interval:** the observed p10 to p90, widened as above.

**`Evaluate(samples)`** sorts by time, fits on the earlier 70% and scores only the later 30%, so no test sample can leak into training (`trainedUntil` is reported). It reports the median absolute error, the median relative error and coverage (how often the real value fell inside the interval), with the counts. With fewer than 5 later samples it says so instead of giving a number.

**Adapters**
- `QuestSamples`: OI03-04 "complete" quests only.
- `TravelSamples`: OI03-05 walking samples only, as actual time divided by the planner's estimate.

## Calibration error, measured

| Fixture | Train | Test | Median absolute error | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stable quest times (400–480 s) | 21 | 9 | under 40 s | reported |
| test set shifted to 2000 s | 21 | 9 | over 1000 s | under 0.5 |

The test asserts the second row's large error, so a shift is shown, not hidden. These are fixture numbers. Your own calibration error appears once your sessions have produced enough samples; the AfterAction report (OI03-11) and the harness (OI03-14) show it from live data.
