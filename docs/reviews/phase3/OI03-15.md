# OI03-15: Retention and privacy controls

**Requirement:** opt-in recording, per-character retention caps, reset, export and delete, and offline support with no provider.

**Acceptance:** switched off, nothing new is recorded; reset and delete remove derived state as documented; bounded persistence and migration tests pass.

## How "reset" and "delete" work here

The owner's data preservation override applies: collected records are never destroyed. "Reset" and "delete" mean that the records go to the archive and a new record begins. What actually goes away is derived, working state only:
- the open activity-timeline segment
- an open trip
- the duplicate filter
- the attribution link to the last Go

## What was built

**Opt-in.** The outcome recorder, which carries the timeline, trips, economy, chronicle and evidence, is off by default (OI03-02). Switched off, it records nothing new from any source:
- event handlers belong to the module and are removed with it
- the tickers stop
- the mail and auction hooks check that the module is on

**Retention caps.** Each character has its own caps (`keep` in the recorder's settings), kept within bounds:

| Store | Default | Bounds |
|---|---|---|
| events | 500 | 100–2000 |
| trips | 200 | 50–500 |
| timeline records | 20 | 5–60 |
| confirmed sales | 200 | 50–500 |
| chronicle | 300 | 100–1000 |

Past a cap the oldest records are archived, never dropped. The chronicle's duplicate index keeps 30 days (OI03-08).

**Start fresh (archived).** Two clicks within 10 seconds. It archives this character's whole record (`outcomesReset`, in the preservation audit), then begins a new one and restarts the working state. If archiving fails, nothing changes.

**Export my records.** All of this character's records as one OTR1 data code in the copy dialog, which reads back with `Serialize.Import`. Nothing is sent, and nothing is said in chat. Export evidence (OI03-14) and Export the chronicle (OI03-08) stay as they were.

**Offline, no provider.** Everything works with no companion and no advice file: the report, the evidence and the exports. The companion stays an optional reader of the saved file (rule 1).

**Migration.** A store from another schema is archived whole before a new one starts (`outcomeEvents`, OI03-02).

**UI.** The After action page has "Export my records" and "Start fresh (archived)", both with tooltips, and the Help entry and USER_GUIDE are updated.
