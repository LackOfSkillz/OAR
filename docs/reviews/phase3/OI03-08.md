# OI03-08: Chronicle timeline

**Requirement:** a concise, chronological record of milestones and decisions, with references to plans and outcomes and with privacy controls.

**Acceptance:** replay and reload keep the order and don't duplicate; the player can clear or export selected history; no raw chat or unrestricted event stream.

## What was built

`Intelligence/Chronicle.lua` is driven by the outcome recorder. It uses the same opt-in switch, and entries are saved in `char.outcomes.chronicle`.

**Sources**
- recorded outcomes (`OTR_OUTCOME`, now fired by `Outcomes:Record`)
- your Go on a plan step (`OTR_NEXUS_ACTED`, which now also carries the step's title)
- play session starts

**Only these milestones**
- **level:** each new level
- **quest:** turned in or abandoned
- **zone:** first visit
- **profession:** reaching 75, 150, 225 or 300
- **gear:** first time on an item
- **death:** each death, with the zone
- **decision:** a plan step you chose, with its plan and step ids
- **session:** a play session started

Money, movement and chat are never entries.

**Order and duplicates**
- Every entry has a key, so a replay, a reload or a duplicate event never adds it twice.
- Entries are filed in time order, so a late one goes where it belongs.
- The key index keeps 30 days. It is an index; the entries themselves stay.

**Privacy**
- **Hiding:** kinds you hide (`chronicleHide`) aren't recorded.
- **Clearing:** `Clear(filter)` works by kind or date range. It archives the matching entries first (`chronicleCleared`) and clears nothing if archiving fails.
- **Exporting:** `Export(filter)` gives plain text for the copy dialog; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Bounds:** the newest 300 entries are kept, and older ones are archived (`chronicleEntries`).

Both datasets are in the preservation audit.
