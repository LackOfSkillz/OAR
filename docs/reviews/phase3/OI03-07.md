# OI03-07: Performance and survival observations

**Requirement:** use only readable, permitted post-fight meter results and supported death and recovery events; store comparable context.

**Acceptance:** an unknown meter, active combat, different builds and missing results are safely excluded; restricted combat metrics are never derived.

## Reuse

- **Fights:** FightMeter already saves Blizzard's own post-fight numbers (`C_DamageMeter`, read only after combat, `char.fights`, archived as `fights`).
- **Survival:** the outcome recorder already logs death and recovery (OI03-02).

## What changed

**FightMeter** now stores a context with each captured fight:
- level
- talent build (points per tree, from the Talents module)
- game build
- item level
- in a group or not
- map and play session

It also counts why fights were left out: "combat still active", "damage meter unavailable", "no readable session", "session values restricted".

**`Intelligence/PerformanceStats.lua`** (derived, no store):
- **Fights:** grouped by level band, talent build, game build and solo or group, giving median, p25 and p75 of your damage per second. That is the meter's own amount for you divided by the meter's own duration; nothing else is computed. Rows are excluded, with the reason, when they have no context (recorded before this task), an unknown build, no readable amount for you, or no duration. A stratum needs 5 fights to count as enough.
- **Compare:** only within the same talent build and game build; otherwise it gives the reason and no number.
- **Survival:** deaths, the time from each death to recovery (median), whether you are still dead, and deaths per hour. The per-hour figure needs at least 30 minutes of non-inactive play (from the activity timeline); otherwise it gives the reason.

## Evidence

- `tests/spec/performance_spec.lua`, 6 tests:
  - a captured fight carries its context
  - no meter, combat, and restricted values: nothing stored, reasons counted
  - strata by build, with old rows and empty amounts excluded
  - respec and patch are not compared
  - survival with and without enough play
  - no combat log in the stats or the meter reader
- Full suite: Lua 1245 passed, 0 failed. Companion, quality, deps and security gates are all clean.
