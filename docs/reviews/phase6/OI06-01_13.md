# OI06-01 to OI06-13: Cartographer and Fellowship

| Task | File | What it does | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| OI06-01 observation schema | `Intelligence/World.lua` `Observation`, `FromDiscovery`, `FromSighting` | One shape for Discovery and Sightings: kind, subject, map, fractional x and y, time, build, realm, ruleset, layer, method, precision in yards, consent, provenance. The position is always the player's (`positionOf = "player"`), with how far off that can be. Ids, coordinates, time, build and a known map (when the client can tell) are validated. Sightings now stamp the build. | world_spec |
| OI06-02 source registry | `World.Manifest`, `Classify`, `RegisterPack` | Each pack manifest gives license, revision, flavor, build and evidence class. A pack without a license or evidence class is quarantined (kept, not indexed). Classic packs are labelled, and their entries are never verified. Older than a rule's minimum build means outdated. Built-in packs are first-party. | world_spec |
| OI06-03 conflicts | `World.Resolve` | Every observation is kept with an audit line. Groups are formed by layer and map, then by distance within precision. Groups are weighed by method, source, age and count. A spoofed or unapproved source weighs 0 and is flagged. A newer group of 2+ observations after everything else on that layer and map is "moved" (the old group is kept). | world_spec |
| OI06-04 rare forecasting | `World.RareForecast` | Gaps come only from a death then alive on the same layer and build. Alive-alive is a sighting; a death with no later sighting is censored. No forecast under 3 gaps; a chance only from 8 or more (the share of past gaps shorter than the time since death). An unknown layer gives no gaps. | world_spec |
| OI06-05 explorer | `World.Unseen`, `Route` | Unseen places are kept apart from other-faction ones and ones removed on Forever. The route goes nearest first through verified places only, labelled as straight lines. | world_spec |
| OI06-06 contribution export | `Intelligence/Contribute.lua` | Off by default; consent through the preview's token; only world fields, coordinates rounded to 0.001, realm left out unless asked; size and row limits. | world_spec |
| OI06-07 import and pack build | `Contribute.Import/Decide/Build/BuildAt` | Decoding never runs code. Size, shape and schema are checked; duplicates dropped; a pending queue; approval by hand. Deterministic builds (same hash); not publishable without a rights review; every build kept for rollback. | world_spec |
| OI06-08 community release gate | `docs/COMMUNITY_RELEASE_GATE.md` | The offline manual workflow is described and working. The design for an online service (authentication, rate limits, moderation, deletion, licensing, policy) is written up; none of it is built, and it is blocked pending authorisation. | (document) |
| OI06-09 Fellowship protocol | `Intelligence/Fellowship.lua` | Opt-in FGOAL messages carry goals, plan picks, ready, level and role. They go over PARTY or RAID, with whispers accepted only from group members. Receiving checks the sender is in the group, the channel, version (newer is noted and ignored), field types and lengths, and a per-sender rate. Revoke sends one message; peers are kept in memory only. | fellowship_spec |
| OI06-10 group planner | `Fellowship.GroupPlan` | Ranked by members helped, then by how often those members were already served. Unshared members are "can't tell if ready". No inventory is inferred; nobody is invited. | fellowship_spec |
| OI06-11 group page | Group plan sub-tab under Sharing | Share or stop, ready or not ready, "We'll do this", and member gaps. Every button has a tooltip. A roster change drops members who left. | fellowship_spec |
| OI06-12 plan references | `Intelligence/PlanShare.lua` | Data-only OTR1 references with source, version, build, classes, levels, expiry and steps. Wrong class or level and non-plain ids are refused; expired or older-build plans are marked stale. Accepting or declining is manual and changes nothing in the game. | world_spec |
| OI06-13 multi-client tests | `fellowship_spec` | Mock: two clients exchange messages; revoke; roster change; 20 shares rate-limited; 50 hostile messages from one sender stay one peer; no public channel or invite anywhere in the source. **The owner's real two-client test is still pending.** | fellowship_spec |
